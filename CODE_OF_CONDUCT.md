# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in MindCare a respectful, inclusive,
and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity,
background, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and technical professionalism.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy, especially when discussing mental-health scenarios or data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Prioritize the safety and wellbeing of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing or mocking a person's mental-health experience.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or collaboration.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through a
GitHub Security Advisory. Include relevant links, dates, and context. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent possible.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this
policy. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the
project publicly.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
